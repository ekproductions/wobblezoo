Privacy

Updated: September 7, 2026

Wobble Zoo is an iPhone game without its own user accounts or game server. This policy covers the app, support correspondence and this website.

Game data on your iPhone

Records for each difficulty, your selected difficulty, and sound and haptic preferences are stored locally through iOS UserDefaults. We do not send this game data to our own server or operate our own gameplay analytics or cloud sync. Storage provides the game functions and preferences you use (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; section 25(2)(2) of Germany’s TDDDG for necessary device storage).

Local data remains until reset or removed by deleting the app. Offloading the app may preserve data. Device backups may contain data, depending on your Apple settings.

Advertising with Google AdMob

The free version shows menu and results banners and occasional ad breaks between rounds. A voluntary video can rescue a round once. Google provides the advertising service; for users in the European Economic Area, this is Google Ireland Limited. Wobble Zoo requests only non-personalized ads, sets the maximum ad content rating to G and disables Google’s Publisher First-Party ID. The game is intended for a general audience.

Non-personalized advertising still processes data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate locations derived from them, app or device identifiers, crash and performance data, ad impressions, video views and interactions. This supports ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and protection against abuse. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access the iOS advertising identifier IDFA. Other technical processing by the advertising SDK can still occur.

Before ad requests, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks privacy status and presents the required consent flow. Processing that requires consent is based on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where device storage is involved, section 25(1) TDDDG. UMP stores your choice so it can be respected. Necessary storage of that choice implements your privacy decision. The consent dialog and Google’s notices explain any additional advertising partners, purposes and legal bases.

Where available, change your decision or withdraw consent for future processing under “Privacy options” in the app’s settings. Declining consent may affect ad and rescue-video availability; starting a new round remains free.

Read Google’s iOS advertising SDK disclosures, its information on non-personalized ads and Google’s privacy policy.

One-time purchase through Apple

Apple handles purchase, payment and restoration through the App Store. StoreKit supplies signed transaction and product information so the app can verify access and remove banners and ad breaks. Refunds or revoked purchase access can remove the unlock. This processing fulfils the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive your card number or other payment instrument details.

Voluntary rescue videos remain available after purchase and still involve the advertising and consent processing described above. Apple manages App Store transaction records and their retention. See App Store & Privacy.

Visiting this website

GitHub hosts this entirely static website through GitHub Pages. It includes no advertising scripts, web trackers, analytics tools or external fonts and sets no cookies of its own. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes other technical request information. Providing and securing the website is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub determines retention of its technical logs; we do not set a separate fixed retention period for them. See GitHub Pages and the GitHub privacy statement. External links contact their destination services only when you follow them.

Email support

When you contact us, we process your email address, message and any information you choose to attach to help with your request. Microsoft Outlook.com provides our mailbox. Please do not send passwords or payment details. Purchase and contract-related requests are processed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; other requests rely on our legitimate interest in responding under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

We delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed to handle the request, unless legal retention requirements or legitimate grounds, such as resolving claims, require continued storage. See the Microsoft privacy statement for the email provider’s processing.

Recipients and international processing

Google, Apple, GitHub and Microsoft may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their linked privacy notices explain responsibilities, retention and applicable transfer mechanisms, such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses. We do not operate our own central database of game, advertising or payment profiles.

Your rights

Subject to applicable legal requirements, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of your personal data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your particular situation. You can withdraw consent for future processing without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Use the app’s privacy options or contact ek_productions@outlook.de.

You may complain to a data protection authority, particularly where you normally live or work or where an alleged infringement occurred. The authority for North Rhine-Westphalia is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information. Without a game account, we cannot identify or delete your local game data remotely; you manage that data on your iPhone.
